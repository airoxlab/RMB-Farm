How to Buy

Buying from a small farm works best person to person. Here’s how it goes.

  1. Call or text

    Reach the farm at 937-605-3352 and tell us what you’re looking for.

  2. Ask what’s ready

    We’ll let you know what’s available now, the price, and when more is coming.

  3. Set a time

    Agree on a time and the details together, then come out to Free Road.

Please call before you visit. This is a working farm with animals to care for, so arranging a time first means someone will be there to help you.
