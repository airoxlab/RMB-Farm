One-page sample site for **RMB Farm LLC**, a livestock farm in New Carlisle, Ohio (Clark County).

## Sections
1. Sticky header with a `tel:` call button
2. Hero (stock barn photo), "Raised on Free Road"
3. Fact strip: Pigs, Eggs, Chicken / family-run / address / phone
4. What We Raise: Pigs, Farm Eggs, Chicken cards, all "call for availability"
5. How to Buy: call or text, ask what's ready, set a time, plus a "please call before visiting" note
6. The Farm: short factual about block with business details
7. FAQ: prices, drop-ins, location, eggs year-round
8. Contact band: call, text, map link
9. Footer: stock-photo credit

Design: Big Shoulders Display + Public Sans; barn oxblood, feed-sack oatmeal, pasture olive, egg-yolk amber. Light/dark aware.

## What was excluded
- The lead keyword "real estate agency": the Google listing says livestock producer, and nothing suggests real estate.
- Family and owner names (from Facebook and people-search sites). None appear on the site.
- Prices, acreage, history, breeds, and pickup or delivery terms: none are published.
- "Open 24 hours" from Google: it is a working farm, so the site says to call first.
- A 2021 Clark County Jr. Fair buyers list showing "Rmb Farm": could not be opened or tied to this business.
- Reviews and testimonials: none exist.
